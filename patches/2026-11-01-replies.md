## #230086（tgymnich 10-08、patrikf 10-27）

一般留言：

> Thanks @tgymnich, fixed. `remainder` goes through negative intermediate values, so a format without a sign now computes it in a signed copy of itself, and a negative remainder is NaN with `opInvalidOp`. >
> @patrikf thanks for the link. This matches the RFC except for `-0.0` to `f8E8M0FNU`: here it loses its sign and takes the substitute for zero with `opInexact`, like `+0.0` does, rather than becoming NaN.
